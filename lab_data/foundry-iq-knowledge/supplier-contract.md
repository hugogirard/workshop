# Supplier Agreement Excerpt — Highland Roastery Partners

> Fictional sample document for the Foundry IQ workshop. No real people, customers or data.

**Contract ID:** SUP-ROAST-118 · **Supplier:** Highland Roastery Partners · **Owner:** Procurement
**Term:** 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 · **Status:** Active

## 1. Scope
Highland Roastery Partners supplies green and roasted single-origin coffee to ContosoMart for
resale under the ContosoMart brand.

## 2. Covered SKUs
This agreement covers the following single-origin lines:
- **Ethiopia Yirgacheffe** (SKU `BEAN-ETH-YIRG`) — washed, grade 1.
- **Colombia Huila** (SKU `BEAN-COL-HUIL`).
- **Guatemala Antigua** (SKU `BEAN-GTM-ANTI`).

> Note: the **Ethiopia Yirgacheffe** best-seller (the SKU that stocked out during the promo in
> Act 2) is covered here — replenishment terms are in Section 4.

## 3. Pricing
Prices are fixed per kilogram for the contract term and quoted in **USD**. Volume tiers apply
above 500 kg per quarter (3% discount) and 1,000 kg per quarter (6% discount).

## 4. Replenishment &amp; lead time
- Standard **lead time: 14 days** from purchase order to warehouse receipt.
- **Priority replenishment** (7 days) is available for an agreed surcharge, intended for
  stock-out recovery on best-sellers such as Ethiopia Yirgacheffe.
- Minimum order quantity: 60 kg per SKU.

## 5. Quality &amp; freshness
Roast date must be within **7 days** of dispatch. Batches failing the roast-freshness window
are subject to the ContosoMart Freshness Guarantee (see POL-RET-032, Section 3).

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice. Outstanding purchase orders are
honored through fulfillment.
