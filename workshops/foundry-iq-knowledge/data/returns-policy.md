# ContosoMart — Returns &amp; Refund Policy (v3.2)

> Fictional sample document for the Foundry IQ workshop. No real people, customers or data.

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01 · **Owner:** Customer Care · **Doc ID:** POL-RET-032

## 1. Standard returns window
Customers may return unopened, non-perishable goods (gear, accessories, unopened bean bags)
within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

## 2. Coffee beans
- **Unopened** bean bags: returnable within 30 days.
- **Opened** bean bags: not returnable for hygiene reasons, **except** under the Freshness
  Guarantee (Section 3).

## 3. Freshness Guarantee
If beans arrive past their **roast-freshness window** (roasted more than 21 days before
dispatch), the customer is entitled to a **free replacement or full refund**, even if the bag
is opened. Proof of roast date is the batch code printed on the bag.

## 4. Subscriptions
- Subscribers can **pause** or **cancel** any time before the monthly cut-off (the 25th).
- A shipment already dispatched follows the standard returns rules above.

## 5. Wholesale partners
Wholesale orders are governed by the partner contract, not this policy. See the relevant
supplier/partner agreement (e.g. SUP-ROAST-118).

## 6. Refund timing
Refunds are issued within **5 business days** of the returned item being received and
inspected. Original shipping is non-refundable unless the return is due to a ContosoMart error.

## 7. Exceptions
Cold-store perishables and clearance items marked "final sale" are non-returnable.
